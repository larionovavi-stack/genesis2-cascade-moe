Genesis 2 — live demo

Ask about Linux, networking or SCADA, in English or Russian. Runs on a CPU, no GPU.

Looking up the current demo address…
The demo runs on a free Kaggle session and its public address rotates roughly every 11 hours, so this page looks up the current one instead of hard-coding it.

Built Russian-first. On held-out queries it has never seen: 80% in Russian (16/20) answered with a command that does the job, 38% in English (11/29). 204 ms median. Ask it in Russian and you will see it at its best. The benchmark that produces those numbers ships with the source and separates real cascade answers from exact-match lookups — benchmark_honest.py.
